Ranking method

Tier A requires both trades advertised by the company and a verified B+C-36 license. Tier B has B+C-36 but drywall self-performance unconfirmed. Tier C has only C-36 verified. Within tiers, documented scope, workers’ compensation/disclosure and service-area evidence break ties. Costs are not scored because no comparable combined quote exists. This transparent ordering avoids invented numeric scores. B classification · C-36 classification · hiring guidance.

BPC §7059 allows qualifying incidental and supplemental work by specialty contractors; dual classification is a screening signal, not the only possible arrangement or proof of job acceptance.
