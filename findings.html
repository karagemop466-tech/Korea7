How the interview order was built, what the access options mean, and what to require in writing for an occupied unit.

Access findings

Manufacturer instructions show plate/linkage service through the overflow opening on documented models, while the overflow elbow/gasket sit behind the tub. That supports trying above-access first only after the contractor identifies the assembly; it does not prove this seized unit can be freed from above. Oatey's replacement test requires inspecting connections behind the tub, so if a ceiling is opened the test/inspection should happen before it is closed. Framing must not be cut without building-official approval.

All-in-one finding

A B + C-36 record is a licensing fit for a single prime contract, but it is not proof the company will include or self-perform drywall. General SF and Handyman Heroes have the clearest reviewed official combined-service pages. Ace, Genteel, Rapid Flow and Manny's need written confirmation of ceiling restoration. The three quoting plumbers are C-36 only on CSLB, so a single plumbing-plus-drywall contract is not established.
