Requested written scope

  1. Inspection and safe above-tub assessment, including a decision point before attempting extraction.
  2. Alternative access plan if required: ceiling opening, protection of the storage space, debris removal and any unforeseen-condition authorization.
  3. Repair or replacement scope, materials, water and overflow leak testing before closure, and warranty.
  4. Drywall patching, texture, painting and cleanup, explicitly stating any exclusions.
  5. Tenant communication, access windows, noise and dust controls, and a single accountable point of contact.
