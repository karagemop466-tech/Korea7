What the plumbing code exempts — the actual wording

SF.gov says a permit is needed before cutting into or replacing pipes, and points to San Francisco Plumbing Code §104.2 for the exemptions. This page publishes that exempt-work list word for word from the City’s own PDF and shows what the City amended — so the test can be checked instead of trusted.

!

Scope of this page. It reports official text. It contains no instruction, request or recommendation about obtaining or not obtaining a permit, and it records no private project rationale. Only the San Francisco Department of Building Inspection can determine whether a specific job needs a permit; sf.gov lists the Permit Center help desk and permitsf@sfgov.org as contacts. Nothing here is legal advice.

Why this page exists

Until 2026-11-10 this site linked §104.2 but quoted none of it — the permits table said “link provided for manual review of exemption wording”. The official wording was read and transcribed on 2026-11-10, so the exemption test can now be checked line by line. The permits page keeps its neutral wording; the quoted text lives here.

The nine exempt-work items, verbatim

Item Official text (verbatim) Could it cover this repair? Why / why not Source
1 Repair of leaks not requiring cutting into or removing piping. No (this project’s reading) A seized trip lever that must be freed or removed is not a leak repair, and concealed waste-and-overflow work is the situation sf.gov describes as needing a permit. S96
2 Unstopping of traps, sewers, vents or waste pipes not requiring cutting into or removal of traps or piping. No (this project’s reading) Covers unstopping a blocked trap, sewer, vent or waste pipe without cutting, not freeing, adjusting or replacing the trip-lever linkage. S96
3 Replacement, repair or maintenance of faucets, angle stops, hose bibbs or roof drain not requiring cutting into or removal of piping. No (this project’s reading) Names faucets, angle stops, hose bibbs and roof drains only. The trip lever, overflow plate and waste-and-overflow assembly are not listed. S96
4 Replacement of toilets except in structures with alternate water piping. No (this project’s reading) Toilet replacement only. S96
5 Replacement of furnace filters. No (this project’s reading) Furnace filters only. S96
6 Removal of drainage, water or gas piping from a building when the water supply, gas and drainage lines have been disconnected in an approved manner under a permit. No (this project’s reading) Removing piping from a building after disconnection under an approved permit, not repairing or replacing a fixture assembly in place. S96
7 Work involved in setting up for display of plumbing fixtures or appliances, when not connected to any supply or drainage lines, in a sales establishment. No (this project’s reading) Display fixtures not connected to supply or drainage lines only. S96
8 Replacement of water conservation devices not requiring cutting into or removal of piping. No (this project’s reading) Water conservation devices not requiring pipe cutting only. S96
9 Work performed in buildings or structures owned and occupied by the State and Federal governments. No (this project’s reading) State- and Federally-owned and occupied buildings only. S96
Transcribed 2026-11-10 from the City and County of San Francisco publication 2025 San Francisco Plumbing Code (PDF), pages 4–5. Wording is unchanged; only PDF line breaks are collapsed. The third and fourth columns are this project’s reading of the quoted text, not a determination — the Department of Building Inspection decides applicability for a specific job.

Amendment scope — and a fee rule often misread as an exemption

Official wording on the page What it establishes Verification Source
… 104.2 Replace Section 104.2(1) and the first paragraph of Section 104.2(2) as follows: The City’s amendment replaces only item (1), item (2) and the first paragraph of item (2). Items (3)–(9) above are the model code text as printed in the same official document; that document does not show they were amended locally. official — City PDF, read 2026-11-10 S96
104.5 Permit Fees. … The replacement of a plumbing fixture or a gas appliance without a change in drainage, piping or venting shall not require the payment of permit issuance fee. A fee provision, not an exemption from the permit itself. It settles nothing about whether this repair needs a permit. official — City PDF, read 2026-11-10 S96
Both quotations come from the same official PDF read on 2026-11-10. No interpretation of any individual job’s circumstances is recorded here.

Official pages read for this page

Official page What it says Read Source
sf.gov — Apply for a plumbing and mechanical permit You need a permit before cutting into or replacing pipes, particularly pipes that will be covered by a wall or buried in the ground. The page then points to §104.2 for exemptions. 2026-11-10 S01
sf.gov — Check if your construction project needs a permit Minor repairs to existing interior plaster or wallboard, unless they are required for fire protection. Listed as work that does not need a building permit — a separate question from the plumbing permit above. 2026-11-10 S02
2025 San Francisco Plumbing Code (PDF) Prints the nine exempt-work items above and states which of them the City amended (§104.2(1) and the first paragraph of §104.2(2)). 2026-11-10 S96
None of these official pages contains a plumbing-specific exemption for repairing or replacing a trip-lever waste-and-overflow assembly. That is a statement about the text of the code, not a determination for any individual job.

What this does and does not settle

Established: the nine exempt-work items in §104.2, the fact that the City amended only items (1) and (2), and the separate sf.gov wording on cutting into pipes and on patching interior wallboard.

Not established: whether the repair in this project falls inside or outside any exemption. No inspection has been performed, the assembly model and the extent of any cutting are unknown, and the answer belongs to the DBI, not to this site. That question stays open and is carried on the Flagged for Review page.
