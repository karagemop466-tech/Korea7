Constraints

  • One trade or one contract: owner prefers an all-in-one service covering plumbing and drywall (ceiling opening + patch).
  • Licensed: contractor must hold an active California CSLB license (owner requirement, independent of the permit decision).
  • Occupied unit: tenants in place — scheduling, access, dust control, and water-shutoff windows matter.
  • Storage unit below: the work area for ceiling access is a usable storage space; contents and access scheduling must be coordinated.
  • Timing: owner wants the work done this week — see flag F4 for official permit rules.
