Overview / Methodology

Methodology & verification rules

How every row on this site was checked, what counts as an official source, and where the limits of this research are.

Rules applied

  1. Every table row links to its sources. No row is published without at least one source link.
  2. Official sources for facts are: California state government (leginfo.legislature.ca.gov), the Contractors State License Board (cslb.ca.gov), City & County of San Francisco (sf.gov), and the companies' own official websites (for what the company itself claims — services, addresses, phones, experience). Code-publisher commentary (IAPMO) is labeled as a publisher reference.
  3. Never infer. If a value could not be confirmed from an official source, the cell is left blank and the item appears on Flagged for review. No blank was ever filled from a third-party directory.
  4. Owner-reported facts are labeled Owner-reported (the symptom, the 5+ plumbers, the storage room, occupancy). They are inputs, not findings.
  5. Retrieval dates recorded: all CSLB records were read from CSLB's own License Detail pages on 2026-11-06 (CSLB prints "Data current as of 11/6/2026" on each record); official sites and Yelp listings were read the same day.
  6. License verification method: the license number shown on each company's official site (or found via the official CSLB page) was looked up directly in the CSLB database at cslb.ca.gov/OnlineServices/CheckLicenseII/LicenseDetail.aspx?LicNum=…, and the business name and address in the returned record were matched to the company before marking it verified.

What was searched

  • The three quoting companies' official websites and their CSLB records (identity + license + classifications + bond + workers' comp).
  • Additional candidates surfaced from Yelp's own search listings for San Francisco plumbing and multi-trade contractors (the owner asked for Yelp and reviewed services), then verified: official site → CSLB record → Yelp listing.
  • Official rule text: sf.gov permit pages, California B&P Code §7048 (current law), CSLB classification pages (C-36, B, B-2), and the §104.2 exemption language via the code publisher.

Limitations

  • Point-in-time data. License statuses, bonds, insurance expirations, and Yelp ratings change. Everything here was current as of 2026-11-06; re-verify links before hiring.
  • Private quotes are unverifiable by design — recorded as client-provided (F7).
  • Yelp automated access was blocked (HTTP 403), so Yelp values came from Yelp's own search-listing snippets; direct Yelp business links are provided for the owner's manual review (F11).
  • No interviews, site visits, or references checks were performed. Inclusion in the provider table is not an endorsement.
  • Not legal advice. The permits page summarizes official public rules with citations; the owner decides the permit posture (F4).
  • No photographs of the fixture were available; the scope was built from the written description (Problem & scope).

Reproducibility

To reproduce this research: open each CSLB License Detail link on the Providers page and compare the returned business name, address, classifications, bond, and workers'-comp rows against the table; open each official-site link and compare the claimed services/addresses; open the sf.gov and leginfo links for the rules. All links are also listed on Sources with retrieval dates. The underlying repo notes and session log are in COORDINATION.md and EXECUTIVE-SUMMARY.md.
