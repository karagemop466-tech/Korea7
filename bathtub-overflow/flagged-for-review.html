F4 Permit requirements for the scope. What official SF sources say about permit coverage of the replacement vs adjustment scopes. SF DBI says a permit is needed before cutting into or replacing pipes, particularly pipes covered by a wall, and points to §104.2. The City's Plumbing Code limits relevant exemptions to work that does not require cutting into or removing piping; the listed items do not specifically name trip-lever adjustment or replacement. The former IAPMO/model-code summary is not used as a San Francisco job determination. Open. Neither Q3 adjustment nor Q1 replacement is classified here: the method and amount of pipe work are unknown. A permit exemption must not be assumed from the quote amount or short scope description. Not legal advice. Ask SF DBI to confirm the exact written scope before scheduling.
