4. Risks and open items

  • Concealed condition unknown. Until the ceiling is open, nobody has verified the pipe material, corrosion state, or whether the lever is replaceable from outside the wall. Mitigation: one contingent written scope (adjust → replace).
  • Occupied unit. Water shutoff, noise, and worker access must be scheduled with tenants. Mitigation: written access windows; firms with PM practice (Fast Response, Bay Area Plumbing, Ace) list this workflow on their official sites.
  • Storage unit below. Contents protection and access scheduling; ceiling patch finish level to agree beforehand.
  • Permits. SF DBI requires a permit before cutting into or replacing pipes, particularly pipes covered by a wall, and points to the local §104.2 exemption list. The short quote and unknown access method do not settle whether a listed exemption applies to this particular adjustment or replacement. Obtain a scope-specific answer from DBI before work (Permits & licensing; flag F4).
  • Insurance gaps on specific licensees. See flags F5 (Advanced Plumbing & Drain future-suspension note) and F6 (General SF workers'-comp exemption).
