Method and limits

The build validates source IDs, cell counts, blank flags, source roles, license-field agreement with the direct-read register, navigation, internal links/anchors and privacy wording. The source ledger records direct reads on 2026-11-08; the automated verifier performs no network check. A contextual official link does not verify an owner statement. Repository-wide inventory and tests are linked on Repo review. Deployment is confirmed separately after merge.
