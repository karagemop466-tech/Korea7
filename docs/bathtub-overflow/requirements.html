Milestone check

Acceptance checklist

  • Written diagnosis and agreed method (above vs. below) approved before destructive work.
  • Written permit determination / DBI confirmation recorded before covered pipe work or access work proceeds.
  • Work area, tenant coordination, storage-area access, protection, and dust/debris containment agreed.
  • Price and responsibility clear for plumbing plus all drywall/paint restoration, including exclusions and return trips.
  • Final mechanism operates, overflow/drain assembly is checked for leaks, and access opening is finished or clearly scheduled.
  • License, insurance, change-order path, warranty, cleanup, and final documents checked.

A checked box means the project owner has confirmed it with the contractor; this research did not check off or complete any field work.

No permit exemption is assumed. The SF DBI page says a permit is required before cutting into or replacing pipes, particularly pipes covered by walls. Code exemptions may apply to some work, but the precise plumbing and drywall scope is not settled here. Confirm with SF DBI at the official guidance page or its listed Permit Center help desk.
