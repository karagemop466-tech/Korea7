Contact script

Questions to ask before authorizing a visit

Capability & access

  • Have you repaired a bathtub waste-and-overflow trip-lever/plunger assembly where the linkage is stuck behind the wall?
  • Would you inspect before deciding whether to remove the trim from above or open the ceiling below? What makes you stop before forcing it?
  • Can your team perform the plumbing and restore the drywall (tape/texture and paint), or would you coordinate a named subcontractor?
  • Can you provide references or photos of similar bathroom access-and-restoration work? (No claim that such examples were found in this research.)

Occupied unit & quote

  • How will you protect tenant belongings, control dust/debris, maintain a usable bathroom, and schedule/communicate entry?
  • Does the written quote separate diagnosis, parts, labor, ceiling opening, plumbing, disposal, drywall finish, painting, and any return visit?
  • What hidden-condition allowance, change-order approval, warranty, and cleanup terms apply?
  • Will you confirm the permit requirement for the proposed work with San Francisco DBI before starting?
Important boundary: License status proves license-record information as of the lookup time; it does not independently prove workmanship, insurance adequacy, service availability, specific experience, Yelp review authenticity, or permit exemption. Request current proof of insurance and verify the contracting name with CSLB.
