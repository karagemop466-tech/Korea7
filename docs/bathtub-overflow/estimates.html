Make bids comparable

Ask each provider to quote the same scope

Request one written, itemized proposal for the following conditional plan (not a diagnosis):

  1. Inspect the overflow trim/linkage and determine whether a non-destructive tub-side removal is feasible; stop if removal risks damage to concealed plumbing.
  2. If needed, locate and create the smallest appropriate access opening from the ceiling below, with dust/debris containment and protection for the storage area and occupied unit.
  3. Repair or replace the waste-and-overflow / plunger mechanism as agreed after inspection; specify exact parts, compatibility, labor, and leak testing.
  4. Identify who patches, tapes, textures, primes, and paints the opening, and whether finishing is included in one visit or requires a separate return.
  5. State exclusions, hidden-condition/change-order process, total price, warranty, scheduling, cleanup, insurance, and responsibility for confirming permit requirements with SF DBI.

This list is a comparison template, not a confirmed job scope. The owner and contractor should clarify the final approach after inspection and compliance review.
