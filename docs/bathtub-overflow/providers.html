Call-screening questions

  • Have you diagnosed or repaired a bathtub waste-and-overflow linkage where the trip lever/plunger is seized or trapped behind the tub?
  • Would you assess it from the tub side first, and what conditions make forcing removal unsafe?
  • Can you inspect from the ceiling below? Will you make the access cut, or do you use a drywall trade?
  • Can plumbing and drywall patch/finish/paint be contracted together? Who is responsible for schedule, protection, cleanup, and warranty at the interface?
  • What written diagnostic fee, minimum visit charge, and not-to-exceed limit apply before the owner approves additional work?
  • What permit/inspection determination applies to the exact scope, especially if concealed piping is cut, replaced, or covered?
  • Can you protect stored property, control dust, coordinate tenant access, and leave the ceiling opening safely covered if work pauses?
  • Can you provide your CSLB number and written scope/itemized estimate, plus a reference for a similar access-limited repair?
