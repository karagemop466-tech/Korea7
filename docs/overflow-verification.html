Executive summary

  • Seven license records are now verified on the official CSLB site (status, classification, bond, workers comp). The other five shortlisted names have no license number on record here and remain UNVERIFIED. No job fit or quote is verified.
  • The three quotes are unverified. Their names match CSLB records, but the quotes do not show a license number, so the issuer is not confirmed. Amounts are as supplied and are not directly comparable, because the stated scopes differ.
  • Permit: sf.gov (checked this session) says you need a permit before cutting into or replacing pipes, particularly pipes covered by a wall. SF Plumbing Code §104.2 exempts only work that does not cut into or remove piping. The described scope appears to fall outside those exemptions. DBI must confirm in writing before work starts.
  • All-in-one (drywall plus plumbing) needs a licensing combination under CSLB rules: C-36 for plumbing, plus C-9 for drywall, or a B license that subcontracts or holds the right trade classification.
  • Drywall: of the verified records, only Rapid Flow and Manny's hold a B license, and none holds C-9 (drywall). Four are plumbing-only (C-36). An all-in-one provider needs C-9 or B, or a written drywall subcontract.

Recommended next steps (owner)

  1. Ask SF DBI in writing whether the described scope needs a permit. Start from the official DBI page and PermitSF portal.
  2. Confirm the license number and status of each candidate on CSLB. Seven are already confirmed on the CSLB record (see the contractors page). Confirm the drywall classification or subcontract arrangement for any all-in-one candidate.
  3. Request written scopes from two or three license-verified providers. Scopes should cover drywall access, restoration, who pulls any permit, and warranty.
  4. Confirm the building type (single-family or not). The owner-builder permit path in §104.1.1 applies only to single-family dwellings.
  5. Get legal advice on tenant access and notice before any work in an occupied unit. This was not researched on this site.
