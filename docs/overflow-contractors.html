How to verify a California plumber's license (official steps).

  1. Go to the official CSLB Check a License tool at cslb.ca.gov.
  2. Enter the license number (fastest) or business name.
  3. Confirm: Status = Active; Classification matches plumbing work — C-36 is the plumbing contractor classification; B (general building) may apply to broader remodels; bond current; workers' compensation on file if the contractor has employees.
  4. California law generally requires a CSLB license for construction work valued at $500 or more (labor + materials).
  5. Review complaint/disciplinary history on the CSLB record before hiring.
