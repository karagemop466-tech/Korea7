Suggested next steps (no vendor is committed).

1 · Re-verify licenses on CSLB

Look up each contractor at the official CSLB Check a License tool. Confirm status = Active, classification = C-36 (plumbing), current bond, and workers' comp. California requires a CSLB license for construction work unless a narrow exemption applies; current law (B&P §7048, as amended by AB 1170, effective 2026-01-01) exempts only work under $1,000 and where no building permit is required — the older “$500” figure is out of date (leginfo).

2 · Confirm scope and access

Ask each plumber whether the overflow can be removed from above (preferred) or whether access from below — opening the drywall ceiling of the storage unit below — is needed. Request an all-in-one quote covering plumbing plus drywall repair/repainting.

3 · Tenant coordination

Tenants occupy the unit. Schedule access in advance, confirm work hours, and confirm who coordinates the plumber and the drywall vendor (or one vendor doing both).

4 · Get written quotes

The three figures on this site are user-supplied estimates from 2026-08-30. Obtain current, itemized written quotes (labor, materials, drywall, paint, disposal) before hiring.

5 · Permit question

SF.gov states a plumbing permit is required before cutting into or replacing pipes, particularly pipes covered by a wall. Confirm with the plumber whether the proposed scope requires a San Francisco plumbing permit and who pulls it.

See Flagged for review for irregularities and unverified items, each with source links for manual review.
