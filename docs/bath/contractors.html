  • Fast Response Plumbing & Rooter — The brief's shorthand 'Fast Response' is assumed to be this company (matching name, city, plumbing trade, CSLB C-36); client should confirm this is the entity that quoted (see Flagged for Review).
  • Ace Plumbing & Rooter — Holds B - General Building together with C36 - Plumbing on the same license (read directly from CSLB). Under B&P Code 7057 a prime contract that requires two or more unrelated trades (plumbing + drywall) is within the B classification's scope, so this firm is structurally suited to a single-contract job that includes opening and patching drywall for below-floor access.
  • Genteel Plumbers — Holds B - General Building together with C36 - Plumbing on the same license (read directly from CSLB); structurally suited to a single-contract plumbing + drywall job under B&P Code 7057.
  • Atlas Plumbing & Rooter — C-36 + A classifications; no B General Building classification, so a combined plumbing + drywall prime contract is outside this license's scope - would need a licensed drywall subcontractor/partner.
  • Heise's Plumbing — Company website advertises handyman/property-repair services including drywall, carpentry and minor plumbing alongside C-36 plumbing - a practical fit for small combined repairs, though the license carries no B General Building classification.
  • Discount Plumbing Rooter Services — CSLB shows $2,000,000 liability insurance on file for this license. Do not confuse with a separate BBB-listed 'Discount Plumbing, Inc.' holding license 1144424 (see Flagged for Review).
