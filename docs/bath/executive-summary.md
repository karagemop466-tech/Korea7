# Executive summary — Bathtub wastewater overflow trip lever (San Francisco)

_Project site: [docs/bath/index.html](index.html) · Live: `/Korea7/docs/bath/` on GitHub Pages · Verified: 2026-11-06_

## Purpose

Collect, organize, and analyze everything needed to hire help for one specific repair: a bathtub
wastewater overflow **trip lever** stuck inside hidden plumbing behind a wall, in a tenant-occupied
San Francisco unit with a storage unit directly below. Every value on the site is source-linked;
anything not confirmable from an official source is blank or labeled client-provided and published on
the Flagged for Review page.

## Situation (client-stated context)

- The trip-lever waste-and-overflow assembly is stuck; its working parts sit inside the overflow pipe
  hidden behind the wall.
- Per the client, **5+ licensed plumbers** could not remove it from above without concern over destroying
  the hidden plumbing (client-stated; unverified).
- Those plumbers recommend working from below; a storage unit below means the access path likely runs
  through the drywall ceiling.
- Requirement: licensed plumber or contractor, ideally **all-in-one for plumbing and drywall**,
  experienced with tenant-occupied units (experience itself unverifiable from official sources → ask for
  references).

replacement) and are not directly comparable. Market context: bathtub plumbing repairs run
$100–$700+ ([HomeGuide](https://homeguide.com/costs/bathtub-repair-cost)); tub/shower drain replacement
$500–$1,025 with drywall/tile removal called out as a major cost driver
([Angi](https://www.angi.com/articles/whats-potential-cost-plumbing-leak.htm)).

## What was verified (2026-11-06)

- **8 candidate contractors**, each read directly from the official CSLB license-detail page, all
  **current and active** with C-36 Plumbing: Expert Plumbing Solutions (977638), Bay Area Plumbing
  (757796), Fast Response Plumbing & Rooter (1024971), Ace Plumbing & Rooter (829071), Genteel Plumbers
  (1025814), Atlas Plumbing & Rooter (868994), Heise's Plumbing (1089561, Taro Group Inc dba), Discount
  Plumbing Rooter Services (1140843).
- **Two hold B General Building + C-36 on the same license** — Ace Plumbing & Rooter and Genteel
  Plumbers. Under [B&P Code §7057](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=BPC&sectionNum=7057)
  (official text), a prime contract requiring two unrelated trades (plumbing + drywall) is within the B
  classification: these two can take the whole job — ceiling opening, plumbing, patch — as one contract.
- Technical/code references pinned to official sources: [Watco trip-lever installation instructions](https://www.watcomfg.com/watco/Instructions/500-501-600TL_Instructions_Bath_Waste_Sheet.pdf)
  (parts and the under-tub/behind-tub access a full replacement requires) and the California Plumbing
  Code / UPC chapter on bathtubs ([2022 CPC reader](https://epubs.iapmo.org/2022/CPC/),
  [2024 UPC reader](https://epubs.iapmo.org/2024/UPC/) — §409 Bathtubs: waste outlet, overflow,
  installation and access).

## Core conclusion

If from-above removal has truly failed with 5+ licensed plumbers (client-stated), the $135 and $550
scopes may not resolve the problem; the **$614 full waste-and-overflow replacement** is the scope aligned
with the described failure — and it is plumbing-only. The realistic end-state is **ceiling access +
replacement + patch**, i.e. two unrelated trades. Cleanest single-contract path: a **B + C-36** firm
(Ace, Genteel — both CSLB-verified); alternative: C-36 plumber plus a separately licensed drywall
contractor under two contracts.

## Recommended next steps

1. Get itemized written quotes that name the access method and who patches the ceiling.
2. Ask the two B + C-36 firms to bid the combined scope; request tenant-occupied-work references.
3. Re-verify licenses on [CSLB Check a License](https://www.cslb.ca.gov/OnlineServices/CheckLicenseII/CheckLicense.aspx)
   on hire day; collect insurance certificates (note Bay Area Plumbing's workers' comp exemption flag).
4. Review the 12 items on the site's Flagged for Review page before signing.
