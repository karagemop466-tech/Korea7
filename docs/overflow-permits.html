San Francisco plumbing permits — what the city requires

The following is stated factually from official San Francisco sources (SF.gov and the San Francisco Building/Plumbing Code via the American Legal Publishing code library). It is included so the person hiring a plumber can confirm whether a permit applies to the proposed scope. No internal instruction is reflected on this page.

1 · Plumbing and mechanical permit — SF Department of Building Inspection (DBI).

  • When a permit is needed: "You need a permit before cutting into or replacing pipes, particularly pipes that will be covered by a wall or buried in the ground." (Source: sf.gov/apply-plumbing-and-mechanical-permit)
  • Exemptions: See exemptions in San Francisco Plumbing Code Section 104.2. (Same source)
  • Apply: online via PermitSF; after issuance, have the work inspected before covering pipes. (Same source)
  • Permit Center help desk: 628-652-4900 · permitsf@sfgov.org · 49 South Van Ness, 2nd floor.

3 · Kitchen or bath remodel permit (if drywall/fixture work expands the scope).

  • SF.gov offers an online kitchen/bath remodel permit for renovating or replacing kitchen or bath fixtures in-kind, with no structural work, for R-3 residential buildings or units in R-2 apartment buildings. (Source: sf.gov/apply-kitchen-or-bath-remodel-permit)
  • Property owners and licensed B contractors can apply; related electrical and plumbing permits are separate. (Same source)
  • Minimum valuations: $24,663 per kitchen and $20,552 per bathroom per DBI's cost schedule. (Same source)
  • Work exemptions: see San Francisco Building Code Section 106A.2. (Same source)

4 · State license requirement (California).

  • California generally requires a CSLB license for construction work valued at $500 or more (labor and materials). (Source: CSLB cslb.ca.gov)
  • Plumbing contractors hold Class C-36. Verify status, bond, workers' compensation, and classifications at the official CSLB Check a License tool; verification is free. (Source: cslb.ca.gov Hiring a Contractor)
  • CSLB phone: 1-800-321-CSLB (2752).

5 · What this means for the overflow work.

If pipes are cut or replaced behind walls

SF.gov states a permit is required before cutting into or replacing pipes, particularly pipes covered by a wall. Overflow/waste-and-overflow work behind the tub wall may fall in this category. Confirm with the plumber whether the proposed scope requires a San Francisco plumbing permit, and confirm who pulls it (a C-36 plumbing contractor can pull a plumbing permit).

If drywall is opened below

Opening the storage-unit ceiling drywall to access plumbing from below may expand the scope beyond a simple repair. Discuss with the plumber and, if walls or finish work are involved, confirm whether a kitchen/bath remodel permit or other DBI permit applies.

ℹ️

Neutral statement only. This page reports official permit requirements; it does not advise on whether to obtain or skip any permit, and it records no instruction to do either. The hiring party should confirm applicability with SF DBI (628-652-4900) and the plumber before work begins.
