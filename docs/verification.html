B. Irregularities flagged for review

F1 - Conflicting leader scores in the brief. The same brief states the current leader is 0.4413 and later 0.3195. Both are unverified here; they cannot both be current. No design decision in this repository depends on either number.
F2 - The nine sites cannot be inspected. Their GitHub Pages hosts are unreachable and the owning account returns 404. Every "reported" score in this repo is therefore a user-reported value, and no uniqueness judgement about them is possible until their rasters are supplied as a registry manifest.
F3 - The metric is transcribed, not organisers' code. The problem description page is unreachable from the sandbox. The implementation here follows two independent public versions and passes hand-worked checks, but the official epsilon, edge handling and truth threshold could differ. Treat absolute values as replicable, not certified.
F4 - "nan" and "zeros" suffixes mean two different fill conventions. Several reported submissions are named -nan, others -zeros. A NaN fill may be rejected by the form ("Predicted values must be in range [0, 1]"). Our writer never emits NaN and the validator fails closed.
F5 - A public third-party raster in submission format contains NaN. The GeoTIFF at kevin9327/DOEGEMSChallenge contains 7.19% NaN and a maximum of 0.94. Whether the organizer tolerates that is unknown; our validator rejects it. This is not an accusation, it is the reason the validator is strict.
F6 - An unreproducible claim was found and not reused. That same repository claims its emitter takes a local score from 0.2728 to 0.8590 (+214.8%). Nothing in the reachable material supports a leaderboard score of 0.86, and the live leader is reported below 0.45, so the number is a local-benchmark figure at best. Nothing in this repository depends on it.
F7 - No competition data means no scientific claim. The features, the labels and the sample grid are behind a login. Every model number here was produced on synthetic data of the same format. It proves code paths; it says nothing about the real geology.

C. What is deliberately not claimed

  • No leaderboard score is claimed, for any submission, ours or the nine sites'.
  • No duplicate check against the nine sites was possible; the registry gate therefore returns UNKNOWN (a block) rather than a pass.
  • No "unique method" claim is made in the sense of novelty search; the claim made is narrower and checkable: no site in the supplied lists names calibrated sparse emission with a Dinkelbach threshold and a per-fold feature-set contest, and the coverage-aware variant that was our novel part is reported as a loss.
