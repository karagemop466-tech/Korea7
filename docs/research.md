### Why the reported GEMSDOE32 result is not a proven recipe

The prompt reports `h33-h33-2-b2-20261004T220000Z-e5eb6e7e-zeros` at 0.2778. The raster, exact method, evaluator, data lineage, site receipt, and comparisons are not available in this checkout. The only defensible comparison is that 0.2778 is the highest user-reported value among the nine named sites in the supplied list. Its method suffixes or name are not evidence of mechanism. A distance-tolerant DTI might reward spatially distributed predictions under some metric definitions, but attributing the score to “dots” or any specific design would be conjecture. Whether a new result can exceed it is unknown; only the canonical holdout can provide a selection estimate, and only an organizer receipt can confirm a leaderboard score.

The user-provided 0.3195 and 0.4413 current-leader values conflict; neither was confirmed from a current official leaderboard page or receipt in this review. Preserve both as unverified claims until resolved.
