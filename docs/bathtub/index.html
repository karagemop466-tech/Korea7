Three things to know before anyone touches the wall

  1. The drywall is not the permit problem — the plumbing is. Patching wallboard is exempt from a San Francisco building permit, but the same section says the separate plumbing permit still applies, and the plumbing code's exemption list does not name bathtub waste-and-overflow work. S11 ↗ S09 ↗
  2. Only one locally-based firm was verified to hold plumbing and building on one licence. Of nine licence numbers checked against CSLB, seven are active and two are suspended. Exactly one San Francisco firm holds C-36 plumbing and B general building together, which is what “all in one service” actually requires. CSLB ↗
  3. The three quotes are not the same job. Two are replacement prices ($550 and $614). One is an adjust-only price ($135). Choosing on price across those three would be comparing a repair attempt with a replacement, and none of them states whether it includes the ceiling, the permit, or the tenant protections. analysis →
