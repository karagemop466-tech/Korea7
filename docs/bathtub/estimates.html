Three numbers that cannot be compared as they stand

All three quotes arrived on 2026-08-30. Two are replacement prices. One is an adjust-only price. None states a licence number, and none states whether the ceiling is included.

Read this before comparing prices

Quoting the $135 against the $550 and $614 would be comparing a repair attempt with a replacement. The cheapest option is only correct if the stop is a mis-adjusted linkage that can be reached and moved from above — and five or more licensed plumbers have already tried exactly that and declined to force it.

Analysis

  • The two replacement quotes are 11.6% apart ($614 vs $550). That is a normal spread for the same trade on different days, and it is far too small a gap to justify choosing on price alone.
  • The $135 adjust quote is 307% below the cheaper replacement quote. That is not a discount, it is a different job. It is only correct if the plunger handle is merely mis-adjusted and can be reached and moved from above.
  • The evidence in the owner's own description argues against the adjust-only scope. Five or more licensed plumbers have already tried to remove the trip lever from above and declined. If reaching and adjusting the linkage were the whole job, that would already be done.
  • All three quotes pre-date the specific finding that removal may require the ceiling below to be opened, so none of them can be assumed to include drywall. That is the single line item most likely to move the final price, and it is missing from all three.
  • None of the three quotes states a licence number. CSLB considers the licence number a required element of a bid and of advertising, so asking for it is both a compliance step and a free way to screen out unlicensed operators.
  • Therefore the correct next action is not to pick the cheapest of the three. It is to send all three the same written scope (see the Ask page) plus the two single-provider firms, and re-quote on identical line items including the ceiling contingency.

What this page will not do

It will not tell you which of the three is cheapest, because two of the three prices are for a different job. It also will not guess a market rate: no official source publishes a price for this work, and the only official number in the area is the permit fee, which is a separate cost and is itself not transcribed here. See FR-04, FR-05 and FR-06.
