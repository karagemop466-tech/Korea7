Reproduced so that every claim is auditable in place. Read them against the live source before relying on them.

S09 · San Francisco Plumbing Code Section 104.2 - Exempt Work

open ↗
104.2 Exempt Work. A permit shall not be required for the following: (1) Repair of leaks not requiring cutting into or removing piping. (2) Unstopping of traps, sewers, vents or waste pipes not requiring cutting into or removal of traps or piping. (3) Replacement, repair or maintenance of faucets, angle stops, hose bibbs or roof drain not requiring cutting into or removal of piping. (4) Replacement of toilets except in structures with alternate water piping. ...

This is the controlling local text for whether this job needs a plumbing permit. NOTE: bathtub waste-and-overflow work is NOT named in the exemption list. Any scope that cuts into or removes the overflow/waste piping therefore falls outside 104.2.

S10 · Apply for a plumbing and mechanical permit

open ↗
You need a permit before cutting into or replacing pipes, particularly pipes that will be covered by a wall or buried in the ground. See exemptions in San Francisco Plumbing Code Section 104.2. Contractors must hold a C4, C16, C20, C36, C54, B, or A license to apply for a plumbing or mechanical permit in general.

DBI's own plain-language statement of when a plumbing permit is needed, and which licence classes may pull one. Confirms C-36 (plumbing) and B (general building) are both accepted licence types for a plumbing permit.

S11 · San Francisco Building Code Section 106A - Permits, and Section 106A.2 - Work exempt from permit

open ↗
106A.2 Work exempt from permit. A building permit shall not be required for the following: ... 10. Minor repairs to existing interior plaster or wallboard, except when part of a fire-resistive assembly. Note: Unless otherwise exempted, separate plumbing, electrical and mechanical permits shall be required for the above-exempted items.

Patching drywall is exempt from a BUILDING permit. The building code's own note states that the separate plumbing/electrical/mechanical permit requirement still applies, so the drywall exemption does not cure a plumbing permit problem.

S12 · 40 CFR 745.83 - Definitions: 'minor repair and maintenance activities'

open ↗
'Minor repair and maintenance activities' are activities, including minor heating, ventilation or air conditioning work, electrical work, and plumbing, that disrupt 6 square feet or less of painted surface per room for interior activities ... where none of the work practices prohibited or restricted by 745.85(a)(3) are used and where the work does not involve window replacement or demolition of painted surface areas.

The exact federal threshold that decides whether the drywall/ceiling opening is 'minor repair and maintenance' (outside the RRP rule) or a covered renovation. Note the two traps: 'demolition of painted surface areas' and the 30-day same-room aggregation rule.

S13 · 40 CFR 745.84 - Information distribution requirements (occupied rental units)

open ↗
if the owner does not occupy the dwelling unit, provide an adult occupant of the unit with the pamphlet, and comply with one of the following: (i) Obtain, from the adult occupant, a written acknowledgment that the occupant has received the pamphlet; or certify in writing that a pamphlet has been delivered ... (ii) Obtain a certificate of mailing at least 7 days prior to the renovation.

Because the unit is tenant-occupied, the RRP pamphlet obligation runs to the tenant as well as the owner. This is a concrete, checkable deliverable to put into the contract.

S14 · Asbestos Demolition and Renovation Notification / Job Number

open ↗
The Air District must be notified at least 10 business days before: Any renovation involving the removal of 100 sq. ft. or more, 100 linear ft. or more, or 35 cubic feet or more of asbestos. Every demolition regardless of asbestos content. For residential buildings of four or fewer dwelling units, you may notify the Air District 72 hours in advance with the payment of an additional fee.

The Air District's own thresholds. A repair-level access opening is far below 100 sq ft / 100 linear ft / 35 cubic ft, so this notification does not appear to be triggered by the described scope. Recorded as a checked-and-not-triggered item, not as advice to skip notification.

S15 · Civil Code Section 1954 - landlord right of entry

open ↗
(a) A landlord may enter the dwelling unit only in the following cases: (1) In case of emergency. (2) To make necessary or agreed repairs ... (d)(1) ... the landlord shall give the tenant reasonable notice in writing of his or her intent to enter and enter only during normal business hours. The notice shall include the date, approximate time, and purpose of the entry. ... Twenty-four hours shall be presumed to be reasonable notice in absence of evidence to the contrary.

The statutory basis for scheduling an occupied-unit repair: written notice with date, approximate time and purpose; 24 hours presumed reasonable.

S16 · Business and Professions Code Section 7159 - home improvement contracts

open ↗
(b) ... 'home improvement contract' means an agreement, whether oral or written ... between a contractor and an owner or between a contractor and a tenant ... if the aggregate contract price ... exceeds five hundred dollars ($500).

Statutory basis for the $500 written-contract threshold, and confirms the statute expressly contemplates contracts made with a tenant.

S17 · US 4,352,213 A - Linkage adjustment arrangement for a bathtub drain assembly

open ↗
Included in this product is a drain tube which leads from the tub drain and connects to a tee fitting. A vertical overflow tube extends downwardly from the elevated overflow port of the tub and connects to the upper end of the tee. A hollow cylinder known as a drop cylinder serves as a valve to control drainage from the tub and is located within the vertical portion of the tee. An adjustable linkage, usually consisting of two connected brass rods and a brass clevis connects the drop cylinder to a trip lever mechanism located at the tub overflow port.

Primary technical description of the trip-lever waste-and-overflow assembly: two brass rods plus a clevis, a drop cylinder inside the tee, and the failure modes of a linkage that is too short (cylinder will not seat) or too long (cylinder cannot clear the port, reducing drain rate). Legal status: expired. Used to explain WHY a corroded linkage can be impossible to withdraw from above.

S19 · Licensed contractors: register yourself or authorized agents on PermitSF

open ↗
We will review your application, verify your license and whether you're listed on your CSLB personnel list, and connect any authorized agents to your license in PermitSF within 2 business days.

DBI's contractor registration step. A contractor must be registered with the City (CSLB number plus San Francisco Business Account Number) before it can pull a permit online, and DBI takes up to 2 business days to verify. Material to a same-week schedule.
