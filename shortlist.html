Shortlist — who can do the plumbing and the drywall

Ranked from the official CSLB records read on 2026-11-07. No provider has been contacted; none has confirmed scope, price, access method or availability.

Why licence classifications decide the shortlist. CSLB's B General Building text states that a general building contractor “shall not take a prime contract for any project involving trades other than framing or carpentry unless the prime contract requires at least two unrelated building trades or crafts other than framing or carpentry, or unless the general building contractor holds the appropriate license classification or subcontracts with an appropriately licensed specialty contractor”. Holding C-36 on the same licence is what lets one firm take the ceiling opening, the plumbing repair and the drywall restoration under one contract.
Source: CSLB — B General Building Contractor · BPC §7057

3. The two-vendor fallback

If none of the four single-contract firms is available or willing, the job can still be done by a C-36 plumber plus a licensed building or drywall contractor, on two contracts or one subcontracted scope. The trade-off is coordination: someone has to own the interface between the plumbing and the finish, and it should be written down.

  • Ask the plumber to subcontract the patch, with the drywall firm named in the quote.
  • Or contract the drywall separately and hold payment until the plumbing has been tested and the ceiling is finished.
  • Never let the two scopes overlap in time without a single person responsible for the opening being closed.
