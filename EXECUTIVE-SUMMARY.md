## 1. Project brief and decision

The reported problem is a stuck bathtub wastewater overflow trip lever in concealed plumbing behind a wall in a tenant-occupied San Francisco unit. From-above removal is preferred, but more than five licensed plumbers reportedly could not remove it from above without concern about damaging hidden plumbing. The reported fallback is access from below through a storage-unit drywall ceiling. The requested service is one licensed provider covering plumbing and drywall, with delicate-work experience in occupied space. These project facts come from the owner’s brief and have no official public confirmation; they are blank/flagged in the [requirements register](requirements.html) and [Project brief](brief.html). No reference pictures were supplied to this session.

**Decision:** shortlist the five firms whose single active CSLB record carries both **B General Building** and **C-36 Plumbing**. This is a documented license-scope fit for one provider handling concealed plumbing plus ceiling restoration, not an official rating of workmanship. The full ranked reasoning, costs, official phone numbers, review links and blank/unverified fields are in the [decision matrix](decision-matrix.html). No provider was contacted by this session; none has confirmed availability, acceptance, a combined scope or a price.

## 7. Recommended next steps

1. Request one contingent written scope from at least three of the five B + C-36 firms, using the same plans/specifications for each.
2. Require the above attempt, below access, ceiling cut/patch/finish, fill-and-inspect test, dust containment and tenant access window as explicit line items.
3. Confirm the actual crew’s workers-compensation and liability coverage on the hire date; four records show no-employee exemptions and one policy expires 2026-12-18.
4. Ask for references on concealed waste-and-overflow work in occupied units; treat company statements and customer reviews as unverified until documented.
5. Re-check the selected license, classification, bond, insurance and complaint disclosure at [CSLB Check a License](https://www2.cslb.ca.gov/OnlineServices/CheckLicenseII/CheckLicense.aspx) on the day of hiring.
