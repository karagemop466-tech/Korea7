Confirm exact scope with DBI. [SF plumbing](https://www.sf.gov/apply-plumbing-and-mechanical-permit) · [§104.2 PDF](https://media.api.sf.gov/documents/2025_SF_Plumbing_Code.pdf) · [Wallboard](https://www.sf.gov/information--check-if-your-construction-project-needs-permit). [BPC §7059](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?sectionNum=7059.&lawCode=BPC) allows qualifying incidental/supplemental specialty work; applicability here is unverified.
- **Tenant entry:** Civil Code §1954 generally requires reasonable written notice with date/approximate time/purpose and normal business hours; exceptions apply. [Official text](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=CIV&sectionNum=1954).
