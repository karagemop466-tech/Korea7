### Six-step discovery protocol before any hiring decision (`P01–P06`)

1. **P01 — Standardize a 5-photo + quote baseline packet** (overflow plate/lever, tub drain shoe, water shut-off valve, storage ceiling directly below, and the three 2026-08-30 written quotes).
2. **P02 — Confirm storage-space access & fire-rating status** (written permission to enter/protect the storage unit below, water shut-off rules, and whether the storage ceiling is a fire-rated horizontal separation).
3. **P03 — Solicit 3 comparable two-stage written proposals** (Stage 1 non-forcing above-first diagnostic $\le \$750$ under [BPC §7159.10](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=BPC&sectionNum=7159.10.); Stage 2 contingent fixed price under [BPC §7159](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=BPC&sectionNum=7159.) itemizing concealed waste-and-overflow + ceiling cut, gypsum/Type X patch, tape, texture, primer, paint, containment, and water test) from dual-licensed leads ([Handyman Heroes #1003394](https://www2.cslb.ca.gov/onlineservices/CheckLicenseII/LicenseDetail.aspx?LicNum=1003394), [Ace #829071](https://www2.cslb.ca.gov/onlineservices/CheckLicenseII/LicenseDetail.aspx?LicNum=829071), [Genteel #1025814](https://www2.cslb.ca.gov/onlineservices/CheckLicenseII/LicenseDetail.aspx?LicNum=1025814)) or a C-36 plumber paired with [Bernal Hill Drywall #995163](https://www2.cslb.ca.gov/onlineservices/CheckLicenseII/LicenseDetail.aspx?LicNum=995163).
4. **P04 — Verify finalist COI, hire-date CSLB status & citation explanation** (current Workers' Comp + Commercial General Liability COI, morning-of-hire [CSLB lookup](https://www2.cslb.ca.gov/OnlineServices/CheckLicenseII/CheckLicense.aspx), 2 occupied-unit references, and written explanation of 2020 citation `2 2020 001045` if considering [#1003394](https://www2.cslb.ca.gov/onlineservices/CheckLicenseII/LicenseDetail.aspx?LicNum=1003394)).
5. **P05 — Confirm post-diagnostic code/inspection path** (stop after Stage 1 if concealed piping resists; have the selected licensed contractor confirm applicable [SF Plumbing Code §104.2](https://media.api.sf.gov/documents/2025_SF_Plumbing_Code.pdf) and building requirements with SF DBI Permit Center `628-652-4900` / `permitsf@sfgov.org` before cutting).
6. **P06 — Execute [Civil Code §1954](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=CIV&sectionNum=1954) tenant entry notice & witness open-ceiling leak test** before closing drywall and releasing final payment.
