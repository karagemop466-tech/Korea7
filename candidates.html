How to weigh it against the top of the ranking

Compared on licensing alone, this record is narrower than the leaders: it carries C-36 with A, C-20 and C-10, but no B or C-9, whereas the top-ranked Ace Plumbing & Rooter holds B alongside C-36 and the second-ranked Genteel record holds B with C-36. On documented evidence of the actual fallback — concealed plumbing followed by wall repair — this is the only firm here with a verifiable published account of it.

The two measures answer different questions. The score answers “which licence best matches a plumbing-plus-drywall job?” The review evidence answers “has anyone described this firm doing something like this?” Neither answers “can they free a seized trip lever without breaking the assembly?” Only a site visit and a written scope can answer that.

Limits, stated plainly

  • The licensed corporation is Sugar Bear Plumbing Inc; the website is Sugar Bear Home Services, LLC. Which entity would contract is unconfirmed.
  • No drywall trade is licensed on the CSLB record. A patch would depend on §7059 “incidental and supplemental” or a licensed subcontractor. No determination is made here — see Permits & licensing.
  • The review evidence is a customer's account on a platform that says licence details are self-reported. It is not an inspection and not a capability finding.
  • No price for this scope is published anywhere, so the price cell is blank and flagged.
  • No one has been contacted. Availability, willingness to work in an occupied unit on this scope, and drywall inclusion are all unconfirmed.
