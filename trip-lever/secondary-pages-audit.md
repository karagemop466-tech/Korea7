# Audit of preserved bathtub-overflow page sets

- **Audit date:** 2026-11-07 (UTC)
- **Scope:** Read-only review of `bathtub-overflow/**` and `docs/bath/**`, including public HTML, data files, generator and tests. The existing alternate pages were not edited.
- **Canonical-page handoff:** The root dashboard has active overlapping work in [PR #42](https://github.com/karagemop466-tech/Korea7/pull/42); this audit does not change root pages.

## Executive result

Both older page sets are still directly accessible on GitHub Pages and show content that is not fully consistent with the current verification rule. They label the owner-reported estimates as unverified, but still display the numeric amounts in tables, cards, comparisons, meta descriptions and public JSON. Review-platform ratings and counts are also rendered even though they are volatile third-party data and Yelp access was restricted during earlier verification. In addition, the two sets disagree about quote dates and which providers are included, and one permit-information page cites a registration page that does not support its stated license list.

**Recommended disposition:** Keep the root dashboard as the only authoritative current summary. Before treating either alternate set as current, blank unconfirmed quote and review values across rendered pages and data, retain only source-backed scope notes, correct the permit citation and qualify candidate capabilities. If the older pages remain published, add an unambiguous historical notice and link to the canonical dashboard. No such remediation was performed in this read-only audit.

## Source-backed provider fit (not a booking or confirmation)

The clearest *advertised* combined-service lead found in the primary pages reviewed is **General SF**: its official building page lists both sheetrock/tape-and-texture and plumbing, and its CSLB record is current and active with B, C-10 and C-36. CSLB also shows a workers’ compensation exemption based on certifying no employees; confirm who would attend and what coverage applies. **Handyman Heroes** is also a lead: its official handyman-services page advertises sheetrock/wall repairs and plumbing; its CSLB record lists B and C-36, with a complaint disclosure that should be reviewed. **Ace** and **Genteel** have active B + C-36 records but the company pages reviewed did not confirm drywall service. None of these sources confirms this specific seized trip-lever job, a careful top-down attempt, tenant-occupied work, a combined price, availability or a restoration commitment. No provider was contacted or booked.

Sources: [General SF — building](https://generalsf.com/building) · [Handyman Heroes — handyman services](https://www.handymanhero.es/handyman-services/) · [CSLB #959843](https://www.cslb.ca.gov/OnlineServices/CheckLicenseII/LicenseDetail.aspx?LicNum=959843) · [CSLB #1003394](https://www.cslb.ca.gov/OnlineServices/CheckLicenseII/LicenseDetail.aspx?LicNum=1003394) · [CSLB complaint disclosure](https://www.cslb.ca.gov/OnlineServices/CheckLicenseII/ComplaintDisclosure.aspx?LicType=LIC&LicNum=1003394).

## Reproducibility and limitations

- Inventory: nine HTML pages in each audited directory. Both sets are directly addressable on the published Pages site; the live estimate pages were fetched during this audit: [bathtub-overflow estimates](https://karagemop466-tech.github.io/Korea7/bathtub-overflow/estimates.html) and [docs/bath estimates](https://karagemop466-tech.github.io/Korea7/docs/bath/estimates.html).
- Local link/anchor audit: **0 broken internal links** across 288 links in `bathtub-overflow/` and 301 links in `docs/bath/`.
- The 12 pure-Python check functions in `tests/test_bath_site.py` were invoked directly and passed. `pytest` itself is not installed in this environment; the audit did not run the site generator because the task was read-only.
- A targeted search of both directories found no directive to bypass permit requirements. The permit observations above are about citation quality and scope-dependent interpretation only.
- The historical pages state CSLB capture dates of 2026-11-06. License status may change; this audit did not re-fetch every provider’s CSLB record. Ace and Genteel’s linked CSLB records were separately re-read on 2026-11-07 for the classification statements above.
- **No root dashboard files were edited.** Open [PR #42](https://github.com/karagemop466-tech/Korea7/pull/42), updated 2026-11-07 20:32 UTC, has overlapping root-page/test scope and describes a correction to the blank-value policy. This branch handed that work off rather than conflict with it; no merge or deployment success is claimed here.
