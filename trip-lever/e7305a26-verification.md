## 3. Official rules, classifications and manufacturer record

- [CSLB B classification](https://www2.cslb.ca.gov/About_Us/Library/Licensing_Classifications/Licensing_Classifications_Detail.aspx?Class=B): a general building contractor shall not take a prime contract involving trades other than framing or carpentry unless the project involves at least two unrelated trades, the contractor holds the appropriate classification, or it subcontracts with an appropriately licensed specialty contractor. This is the basis for preferring one license carrying both B and C-36.
- [CSLB C-36 classification](https://www2.cslb.ca.gov/About_Us/Library/Licensing_Classifications/Licensing_Classifications_Detail.aspx?Class=C36): plumbing contractors provide for a safe water supply and proper disposal of fluid waste, including maintenance and replacement of the described items.
- [CSLB hiring guidance](https://www.cslb.ca.gov/Consumers/Hire_A_Contractor/Finding_The_Right_Contractor.aspx): check license status, obtain at least three written bids on identical plans/specifications/scope, do not automatically accept the lowest bid, verify business address and telephone number, and verify workers-compensation and liability coverage.
- [SF.gov plumbing/mechanical permit](https://www.sf.gov/apply-plumbing-and-mechanical-permit): a permit is needed before cutting into or replacing pipes, particularly pipes covered by a wall; permit fees depend on scope; qualifying license classes include A, B, C-4, C-16, C-20, C-36 and C-54; work must be inspected before pipes are covered.
- [SF.gov permit-exemption information](https://www.sf.gov/information--check-if-your-construction-project-needs-permit): minor repairs to existing interior plaster or wallboard are listed among projects that do not need a building permit, subject to stated exceptions.
- [California BPC §7048](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=BPC&sectionNum=7048): the current exemption is for aggregate contract price under $1,000, casual/minor/inconsequential work, and work that does not require a building permit; amended by Stats. 2025 ch. 67, effective 2026-01-01. Applicability to a specific scope is not decided here.
