Plumbing permit requirement “You need a permit before cutting into or replacing pipes, particularly pipes that will be covered by a wall or buried in the ground.” Replacement of concealed waste-and-overflow piping is the scope described by the rule official — sf.gov S01
Exempt-work pointer SF.gov points to San Francisco Plumbing Code Section 104.2 for exemptions; the official code library hosts Section 104.0 Permits Link provided for manual review of exemption wording official S01 · S03
Work exempt from a building permit SF.gov lists “minor repairs to existing interior plaster or wallboard, unless they are required for fire protection” among work that does not need a permit Relevant to the ceiling-opening / patching discussion official — sf.gov S02
Who may apply for a plumbing permit Contractors must hold C-4, C-16, C-20, C-36, C-54, B or A classifications; scope may be further limited by license type Matches the requirement for a licensed plumber or contractor official — sf.gov S01 · S05
Inspection before covering After a permit is issued, work must be inspected before covering pipes Sequencing consideration if a ceiling is opened and re-closed official — sf.gov S01
Homeowner route Only owners of stand-alone single-family dwellings (R-3) may apply to do the work themselves Tenant-occupied multi-unit context should be checked directly with SF DBI official — sf.gov S01
Small-job licensing exemption B&P §7048 exempts work only when the aggregate price is under $1,000, the work is casual/minor/inconsequential, and no building permit is required (as amended by AB 1170, effective 2026-01-01) Current statutory text; widely repeated older $500 figures are outdated official — leginfo S13
Confirm applicability SF.gov lists the Permit Center help desk and permitsf@sfgov.org as contacts The hiring party should confirm permit applicability for the exact scope with SF DBI and the contractor official — sf.gov S01
