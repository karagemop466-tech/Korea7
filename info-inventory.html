SC01 How can the owner authorize a cautious above-first diagnostic or adjustment visit without committing to open the ceiling? Under California BPC §7159.10, a buyer-initiated Service and Repair Contract of $750 or less requires a signed, dated written contract before any work starts (with license number and CGL insurance notice) and prohibits collecting any payment until the contracted diagnostic/repair work is completed. — UNVERIFIED

SC02 Which statutory contract protects the owner if concealed waste-and-overflow replacement plus ceiling restoration exceeds $500 / $750? California BPC §7159 requires a written Home Improvement Contract when labor and materials exceed $500 (outside §7159.10): down payment is capped at 10% or $1,000 (whichever is less), extra work requires a signed written change order, and under §7159(a)(5)(B) the prime contractor is administratively responsible for completing the entire contracted scope. — UNVERIFIED
No Stage 2 Home Improvement Contract price or scope drafted		 S408 · S224

SF.gov exempts 'Minor repairs to existing interior plaster or wallboard, unless they are required for fire protection.'
