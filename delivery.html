Rules and definitions behind the ranking and the permits page

Rule or definition What the official page states How this project uses it Verified Source
C-36 Plumbing Covers means of safe water supply and proper disposal of fluid waste, including complete removal of waste and maintenance/replacement of those items. Authority cited: B&P §§7008, 7059. Establishes that concealed waste-and-overflow work is within the plumbing trade. 2026-11-10 S05
B General Building (B&P §7057) B&P §7057(b): a B contractor may not take a prime contract for trades other than framing/carpentry unless the contract requires at least two unrelated trades or the contractor holds the appropriate specialty classification or subcontracts with one. §7057(c): C-16 and C-57 require the specialty license. A B licence alone does not authorise plumbing trade work; it needs the specialty class or a subcontract. This is why "B + C-36 on one licence" is the all-in-one test rather than "B alone". 2026-11-10 S83
