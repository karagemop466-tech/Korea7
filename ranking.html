Cost per rank — what is and is not known

  • Ranks 1–6 (dual-classified): no quote exists; the cost cells are blank and marked unverified. The only published price signal is Genteel's $149 dispatch fee credited to the work (company statement). Manny's advertises 5% / 10% discounts (company statement).
  • Ranks 7–9 (owner-held quotes, unverified): Fast Response $135 repair/adjust (adjustment only), Expert Plumbing Solutions $614 full waste-and-overflow replacement, Bay Area Plumbing $550 lever + spout/diverter (different scope). See Estimates & costs.
  • Benchmarks (publisher, 2026): bathtub/shower drain replacement $500–$1,020, average $700; new drywall ≈ $2,100 (Angi); bathroom leak repair $150–$600 with $300–$930 drywall/tile follow-up and $50–$200 call-out fees (Angi). None of the three quotes includes drywall, so a from-below job will cost more than any quote on file.
  • Why the full quote (Expert, $614) does not win: it is a plumbing-only scope from a C-36-only firm; the brief's one-provider requirement and the ceiling access carry more weight than a known price for part of the job.

Sensitivity — what would change the order

  • If only Genteel's 17-review Yelp listing is current, Genteel scores 80 and swaps with Handyman Heroes (U24).
  • If a C-36-only firm supplies a written subcontract with a C-9 drywall contractor (e.g. Bernal Hill Drywall #995163) and C1 is treated as satisfied for comparison, Fast Response would score 94 (rank 1), Expert 87 and Bay Area 81 — but the owner would then be managing two firms and two warranties, which is what the brief asked to avoid.
  • If Fast Response's workers'-comp policy is not renewed by 2026-12-18, its C2 falls to 5 and it scores 59, dropping behind Expert (U28).
  • Setting C6 and C7 (self-statements and third-party reviews) to zero puts Genteel (73) one place ahead of Ace (70) — the published dispatch fee is the only remaining difference — and leaves General SF and Rapid Flow tied at 60; the six dual-classified firms still occupy ranks 1–6.
