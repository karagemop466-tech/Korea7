  • CSLB license verified on the official lookup (status active; check complaint disclosure).
  • Written itemized quote with plumbing and drywall/finish separated.
  • Confirmed experience with below-access waste-and-overflow replacement.
  • Confirmed dust containment, water-shutoff window, and daily cleanup plan (occupied unit).
  • Confirmed ceiling finish scope: patch, texture, paint — in-house or named partner.
  • Confirmed access plan for the storage unit below the tub.
