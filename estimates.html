Analysis

The $135 report is an adjustment-only scope; $550 is a plug/spout diverter scope; $614 is waste-and-overflow replacement. Comparing them as if they were the same job would be misleading. Publisher benchmarks are national/trade context: drain replacement $500–$1,020, leak repair $150–$600 plus $300–$930 follow-up, overflow component $163. Genteel publishes a $149 dispatch fee applied to services, but that is not a repair quote (company FAQ).
