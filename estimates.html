What none of the quotes includes

  • Opening the ceiling. No quote prices the access cut in the storage-unit ceiling.
  • Patching and finishing the ceiling. Drywall, tape, sand and paint to a matched finish.
  • Protection of an occupied building. Dust barrier, floor coverings, notice to the tenant, daily clean-up.
  • Contingency for hidden damage. Corrosion, a previous repair, or water damage behind the wall.
  • Disposal. Removal of old material from the premises.

Ask each firm to price those five lines on the same quote. Until that exists, the three figures above are not comparable to one another. The scope checklist is on the Hiring plan page.
