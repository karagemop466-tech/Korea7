Bathtub wastewater overflow trip lever — stuck behind the wall

The trip lever is seized inside the hidden waste-and-overflow plumbing behind the wall. Removing the overflow from above has failed with 5+ licensed plumbers; below access through the storage-unit ceiling drywall is the reported fallback. This dashboard organizes the three 2026-08-30 estimates, nine contractor candidates (eight with CSLB-verified active licenses, including five that can do plumbing and drywall under one license), independent cost benchmarks, a ranked shortlist with a vetting script, and every item that still needs your review.

How verification works on this site

✅

Every table row has a source link. License records come from the official California Contractors State License Board (CSLB) database with the data-current date printed on the record. Review ratings link to the business's Yelp page. Cost benchmarks link to the publisher's own article.

⚠️

If a value could not be confirmed from an official source it is left blank or marked unverified and flagged — nothing is inferred or filled in. The three estimate amounts are owner-provided and appear with an "unverified" pill; Yelp ratings are third-party and are never counted as license verification. See Flagged for Review.
