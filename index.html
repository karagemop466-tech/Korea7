The problem, in brief

  • What is stuck: the bathtub wastewater overflow trip lever — the plunger-and-linkage assembly behind the overflow plate (industry name: trip-lever bath waste, see Watco's official instructions and Moen's drain guide).
  • Why it is hard: the assembly is inside the hidden plumbing behind the wall. Standard removal is from above through the overflow plate, but forcing a corroded/stuck assembly risks breaking the plunger rod off inside the piping (Lowery Plumbing, Mr. Rooter). 5+ licensed plumbers have already declined to force it.
  • Accepted fallback: work from below. The drain shoe, tee and overflow elbow sit under and behind the tub (Home Depot), reachable by opening the drywall ceiling of the storage unit below. Industry guidance confirms full waste-and-overflow replacement usually requires access from behind or below, and the ceiling repair is a separate cost line (InspectandTest, Angi 2026).
  • Constraint: tenants occupy the unit — this is delicate work in a sensitive, occupied environment, and a single outfit should cover both plumbing and drywall/ceiling finish.

Recommended next steps

  1. Schedule on-site evaluations with 2–3 candidates. Suggested shortlist: one all-in-one (General SF or Handyman Heroes) plus one plumbing specialist (Fast Response or Expert Plumbing Solutions) so the below-access plumbing scope and the ceiling finish scope can be compared.
  2. Ask each candidate the same three questions: (1) Will you attempt removal from above first, and at what point do you stop? (2) What is your below-access plan — exact ceiling opening size, dust containment, and finish schedule? (3) Is the drywall/texture/paint included in your price, or by a partner?
  3. Get written, itemized quotes separating plumbing labor/parts from drywall/finish, and confirm the CSLB license on the official lookup before signing anything.
  4. Plan tenant coordination: water shutoff windows, storage-unit access below the tub, dust barriers, and daily cleanup — the unit is occupied.
  5. Keep the evidence chain: add signed quotes and the chosen provider back to this dashboard (Estimates and Providers pages) so the record stays complete.
