Bathtub wastewater overflow trip lever — hard-to-access repair

A stuck bathtub overflow trip lever inside concealed plumbing behind a bathroom wall in a tenant-occupied San Francisco unit. Five or more licensed plumbers (owner-reported) declined to remove it from above, and working from below means opening the drywall ceiling of the storage unit underneath. This dashboard collects the verified requirements, estimates, licensed providers, official permit and licensing rules, and analysis — every table row carries its source links.
