How verification works here

Licences come from the authority. Status, classification, bond and workers'-compensation entries were read on the California Contractors State License Board record for each licence number on 2026-11-07. Each record is linked in place, and the link can be re-checked in seconds.

If a value cannot be confirmed, it is left blank and flagged. The three quote amounts are owner-provided: they are shown exactly as supplied, tagged, and the official-confirmation column stays empty. One candidate has no licence number at all, so that cell is blank rather than guessed. See Flagged for review.

Reviews are snapshots, not evidence. Star ratings and review counts were captured on 2026-11-07 and drift daily; Yelp also blocks automated reads, so the number is a dated snapshot of the linked page. Licence status, not popularity, decides whether a firm may take the job.
