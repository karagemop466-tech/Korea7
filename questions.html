What still has to be answered

A hiring decision needs answers to these questions first. Each row states the question, why it changes the decision, who can answer it, and how to get the answer. Where no source answers a question the verified-answer cell is left blank and flagged — it is never filled in by inference.

i

How to use this page. Read the question register first. Then the gap table, which groups the same questions by what kind of information is missing. Then the suggested discovery sequence, ordered by cost and by what unblocks the most other answers. The 2026-11-11 re-read tables at the bottom record what was checked today, so the age of each verified fact is visible.

A handle adjustment (the $135 scope) and a full waste-and-overflow replacement (the $614 scope) are different jobs. What this page does not do

It does not answer the questions, and it does not rank the contractors a second time — the ranked hiring decision stays on its own page. It does not conclude whether a permit is required: only the San Francisco Department of Building Inspection can decide that for a specific job, and Q07 records that the question is open. It does not contact, book or price any provider.
