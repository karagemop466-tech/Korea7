A06 Is minor interior wallboard repair listed among work that does not need a building permit? Yes — SF.gov lists minor repairs to existing interior plaster or wallboard among work that does not need a permit. The plumbing scope is governed by the plumbing-permit rule. official — sf.gov S02 · S01
