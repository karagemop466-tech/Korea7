Executive summary

What needs to happen

As reported by the owner, the bathtub overflow trip lever or plunger is stuck behind the wall in a tenant-occupied unit. More than five licensed plumbers have expressed concern about forcing it from above. Access below may require opening the ceiling of a storage unit. These project facts are owner-supplied and have not been independently verified.

Seek one accountable licensed provider for plumbing and access/restoration. Ask for a careful top-down assessment first, and a clear stop point if hidden plumbing is at risk. If below access is necessary, agree on the opening, tenant and storage protection, plumbing repair, leak testing and ceiling restoration before work.
