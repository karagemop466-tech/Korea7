  4. Drywall partner: Bernal Hill Drywall Inc, CSLB #995163, (415) 533-8274 — C-9 + B, insured; not a plumber.
  5. Request at least three written bids on identical scope: above attempt, below fallback, ceiling cut/patch/texture/paint, leak test, debris removal, permits/inspections and occupied-unit protection.

6. Official rules (neutral)

sf.gov states a permit is needed before cutting into or replacing pipes, especially pipes covered by a wall, and work must be inspected before covering pipes (plumbing permit guidance). Minor interior wallboard repairs are listed separately as exempt work unless fire-protection required (permit checklist); that does not establish a plumbing exemption. For R-2 apartment buildings, owners must hire contractors for plumbing work (kitchen/bath permit guidance). Current BPC §7048 is the small-job licensing exemption, not permit-bypass advice (leginfo). Confirm exact scope with SF DBI and the selected contractor.
