Overview · updated 2026-11-08

Executive summary

Decision-ready summary of the access problem, estimate limits, contractor evidence and next steps.

Recommendation

Start with General SF (CSLB #959843) and Handyman Heroes (CSLB #1003394) because they have the clearest reviewed official pages advertising both plumbing and sheetrock/wall repair under a B + C-36 license. Then interview Ace Plumbing & Rooter (CSLB #829071) for its broad license set, current workers' compensation record and property-manager workflow. This is an interview order, not a guarantee; no provider has been contacted or accepted the job.

What is verified

Ten CSLB records were re-read on 2026-11-08: nine ranked candidates plus Sugar Bear Plumbing Inc. Six ranked candidates carry both B General Building and C-36 Plumbing on one record. SF.gov requires a permit before cutting/replacing covered pipes; the Plumbing Code exemptions are limited to work not requiring cutting/removal; minor wallboard repair exemption does not apply when required for fire protection. BPC §7059 allows qualifying incidental/supplemental work by specialty contractors, but applicability here is unverified. Oatey and Watco describe the trip-lever mechanism and post-replacement fill-and-inspect test.

What is not verified

The installed model, seized component, pipe material, ceiling fire-rating, need/size of a ceiling opening, job-specific occupied-unit experience, single-contract drywall inclusion, availability and all-in price remain blank/unverified. The three owner-reported amounts ($614, $550, $135) are not official quotes and describe different scopes. Yelp direct fetches were blocked; numeric ratings are not used.

Cost decision

Do not choose by the lowest reported amount. The $135 figure is an adjustment scope, $550 mentions a spout/diverter that may be a different mechanism, and $614 mentions waste-and-overflow replacement. None confirms ceiling opening, patch/texture/paint, tenant protection, testing or permit/inspection responsibilities. Require identical written scopes; national Angi ranges are context only. See Estimates & costs.

Next steps

  1. Use the ranked interview order and official phone links.
  2. Require one written scope using the occupied-unit checklist.
  3. Recheck CSLB status/disclosures and attending-crew insurance on the hire date.
  4. Have the contractor and DBI determine permit/inspection applicability for the exact scope.
  5. Review Flagged for Review before hiring.
