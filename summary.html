More than five licensed plumbers reportedly could not remove it from above without damage concern; the reported fallback is below access through a storage-unit ceiling. 5. Official rules and next action

SF DBI requires a permit before cutting into or replacing pipes, subject to §104.2 exemptions; minor wallboard repairs have a separate fire-protection limitation. Confirm the exact scope with DBI and the contractor. SF plumbing guidance · §104.2 PDF · wallboard guidance. Request one written scope covering above assessment, below access if needed, ceiling cut/patch/finish, dust/occupant protection, testing before closure, insurance and tenant scheduling. Re-check the chosen license on the hire date. CSLB lookup.
