Executive summary

What is established, what is assumed, and what to do next — in one page. Verified 2026-11-07.

The situation

The trip lever on the bathtub overflow is seized inside the concealed waste-and-overflow plumbing. Access from above has already failed: the owner reports more than five licensed plumbers declined to force it for fear of destroying the hidden assembly. The reported fallback is access from below, through the ceiling of the storage unit, which means opening and later patching drywall in a building with tenants in place.

Three quotes were received on 8/30/26: $135 to repair or adjust the plunger handle, $550 to replace the lever, plug and spout with a diverter, and $614 to replace the waste and overflow. All three companies are licensed and active on CSLB. None of the three quotes includes the drywall work, and none is a combined plumbing-plus-drywall price.

The amounts are owner-provided and cannot be confirmed from any official source. They are reproduced exactly as supplied and tagged. Flagged item F2.

Recommendation

  1. Send one identical written scope to the four B + C-36 firms.
    Use the scope checklist on the Hiring plan page. Identical scope is what makes the bids comparable; CSLB recommends at least three written bids.
  2. Ask each firm how it will get at the assembly before discussing price.
    A firm that has done this work will describe the access route unprompted. Make the route, the cut location and the cut size part of the quote.
  3. Price the drywall as its own line on the same contract.
    Published 2026 figures put drywall at roughly $2,100 when a wall is opened, and $300–$930 for patching — a material addition, not a rounding error.
  4. Verify the licence number on the written bid on CSLB, and read the complaint disclosure if there is one.
    Do this for whichever firm you choose, even the one recommended by someone you trust.
  5. Keep the payment inside the legal limit.
    CSLB: no more than 10% of the contract price or $1,000, whichever is less, as a down payment unless a blanket performance and payment bond is on file.
