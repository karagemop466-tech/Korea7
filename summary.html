Executive summary

The important details of the bathtub wastewater overflow trip lever repair — problem, quotes, who can do the work, what it should cost, and what still needs review. Updated 2026-11-06.

1. The problem

The bathtub's waste-and-overflow trip lever is stuck inside the hidden plumbing behind the wall. The overflow plate sits in a hard-to-access area, and freeing the seized linkage risks damaging the concealed waste-and-overflow assembly. More than five licensed plumbers have already declined to remove it from above for that reason and recommended working from below instead — but the space below is a storage unit, so the fallback plan is opening the drywall ceiling to reach the plumbing. The unit is tenant-occupied, so the work must be coordinated, contained, and handled by one provider who can do both the drywall and the plumbing. Removing the overflow from above remains preferred if it can be done safely.

4. Key findings

  • Below-access is the documented norm when above-access fails. Trade and guide sources say a full waste-and-overflow replacement usually requires access from a panel, the ceiling below, or the wall behind the tub; front-only work is limited to the plate/gasket. Sources: This Old House (2026-06-01), Horow guide (page dated 2026-01-05), The Overall Plumber (2026-09-10).
  • One company can cover drywall + plumbing under a single license: General SF holds CSLB #959843 with B General Building + C-36 Plumbing (+ C-10 Electrical); Handyman Heroes holds #1003394 with B + C-36. Sources: CSLB #959843, CSLB #1003394.
  • All three quoting companies are actively licensed C-36 plumbers with $25,000 bonds on file at CSLB as of 2026-11-06 (links in section 3).
  • The quotes bracket the national benchmark: $135 (adjust) < Angi's $500–$1,020 replacement range < $550/$614 quotes inside it — with drywall/ceiling repair costed separately (~$2,100 new drywall figure; $300–$930 for post-leak patching). Sources: Angi drain-replacement cost (2026-09-18), Angi leak-repair cost (2026-10-28).
  • Occupied-unit planning: with clear access a full waste-and-overflow swap is a 2–4 hour job (4+ hours if drywall must be cut), and the standard stress test fills the tub to check the drain and overflow connections, then inspects every joint under flow conditions — schedule access to the space below the tub for testing. Sources: The Overall Plumber (verified 2026-11-06), Oatey — trip-lever W&O kit (manufacturer).
