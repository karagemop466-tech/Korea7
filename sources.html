How to read tiers

Official sources are government issuers (CSLB, SF.gov, California Legislative Information). Manufacturer sources are product makers (Oatey, Watco). Company sources are self-published business pages. Platform sources are review/discovery directories. Publisher sources are cost/technical articles. Primary sources are this project's own records. A link supports only what its note says; it does not verify unrelated owner facts.
