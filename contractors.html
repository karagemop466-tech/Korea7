Re-verify any licence yourself in 30 seconds

  1. Open CSLB — Check a License.
  2. Enter the licence number from the table above (or the business name).
  3. Confirm the status is “current and active”, that the classification matches the work, that a contractor's bond is on file, and note the workers'-compensation entry — insured or exempt.
  4. If the record shows complaint-disclosure information, open it and read it.
