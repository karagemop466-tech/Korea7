How to re-verify before hiring

  1. Open CSLB Check a License and enter the license number.
  2. Confirm current and active status, classification scope, bond, workers compensation and any additional status/complaint disclosure.
  3. Request certificates for workers compensation and general liability; an exemption is not general liability coverage.
  4. Ask for recent concealed waste-and-overflow references in occupied San Francisco units and a written identical-scope bid.
