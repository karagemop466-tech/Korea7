How to re-verify any license yourself (30 seconds)

  1. Open the official lookup: cslb.ca.gov → Check a License.
  2. Enter the 7-digit license number from the tables above (or the business name).
  3. Confirm Status = current and active, the classification matches the work (C-36 plumbing, B building), the bond is present, and workers' compensation is insured — or note an exemption.
  4. Check the complaint-disclosure section on the record before signing anything.

CSLB's own guidance: verify status, bond, workers' comp, and classification on the official record — Check a License. This site's records were captured 2026-11-06; statuses can change.
