How to re-verify a license

  1. Open CSLB Check a License and enter the 7-digit number from the table (or the business name).
  2. Confirm current and active status, that the classification covers the work (C-36 Plumbing), the bond, and workers' compensation coverage.
  3. Open the complaint-disclosure section of the record before signing anything — see the official disclosure for #1003394.

CSLB's own page recommends reading its hiring guidance and checking complaint disclosure before hiring. Records on this site were read 2026-11-07; statuses change.
