Second independent re-read, 2026-11-10

A separate session opened every published official record again and compared it line by line with what this site already shows. Nothing changed, and that is the finding — but it is a finding about the records, not about any contractor's ability to do the work.

✓

Result: no field-level drift. All sixteen CSLB licence records printed the same entity, issue date, expiry date, status and classifications as the published data, and each page carried a “Data current as of 11/9/2026” database timestamp. The eight rule pages below were read at the issuing agency.

!

What this does not mean. A current licence does not show that a firm has ever freed a seized trip lever, that it will take on the ceiling opening and its repair under one contract, that it is available, or what it would charge. No contractor was contacted for this re-check, and the three quoted amounts on this site remain owner-provided and unverified.

Business & Professions Code §7048 The licensing exemption applies only where the aggregate contract price is under $1,000, the work is casual/minor/inconsequential, AND the work does not require a building permit. It does not apply where the person advertises as a contractor or employs others. All three quoted prices ($135, $550, $614) are below $1,000, but the price test is only one of the conditions and this site does not determine the others. Full subdivision text read 2026-11-10. S162
Business & Professions Code §7059 A specialty contractor may take and execute a contract using two or more crafts or trades where the other work is “incidental and supplemental” to the craft for which the contractor is licensed. Relevant to a C-36 plumber patching drywall as an incidental part of the repair; whether a ceiling opening and its restoration is “incidental and supplemental” has not been determined here. Text read 2026-11-10; no determination made. S163
California Civil Code §1954 A landlord may enter to make necessary or agreed repairs; except in an emergency, entry is during normal business hours with reasonable written notice stating date, approximate time and purpose; 24 hours is presumed reasonable. Bears on giving the tenant notice before a contractor visits an occupied unit. Full text read 2026-11-10. S168
CSLB C-36 classification A C-36 plumbing contractor provides a safe water supply and proper disposal of fluid waste, including maintenance and replacement of the items described. Confirms C-36 is the plumbing classification; it does not certify skill on a seized concealed assembly. Definition read 2026-11-10. S161

Method

Each licence page was opened individually at www2.cslb.ca.gov and the printed fields were transcribed into data/canonical/reverify-2026-11-10b.json. The published values in cslb_records.json were then compared field by field. The comparison covers the business name, entity type, issue and expiry dates, status, classification list, business phone and address; bond, workers'-compensation and complaint-disclosure lines were also compared. All matched.

Two limits are recorded rather than papered over. First, review platforms were not re-read: Yelp refuses automated requests from this environment (HTTP 403), so no rating on this site is presented as fresh. Second, a licence record is a licensing fact and nothing more — see U49.
