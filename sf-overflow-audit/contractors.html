2. California Contractor Licensing Rules Governing Plumbing + Drywall

Official California Business & Professions Code and CSLB classification rules verified 2026-11-07
Statute / Classification Official Verified Rule Impact on Occupied-Unit Overflow & Ceiling Repair Status Official Source
CA BPC § 7048 (AB 1170, eff. Jan 1, 2026)
Minor-work exemption threshold ($1,000)		 Exempts casual/minor work only when aggregate labor + materials is under $1,000, work is not split across contracts, contractor does not advertise as a contractor or employ helpers, and no building permit is required. A CSLB-licensed contractor is required for advertised plumbing/drywall trade contractors and multi-trade concealed repairs. Earlier repo citations of '$500' are outdated pre-2026 law. VERIFIED CA BPC § 7048 (LegInfo)
CA BPC § 7057 & CSLB Class B
General Building Contractor multi-trade authority		 A Class B contractor may take a prime contract involving at least two unrelated building trades/crafts other than framing/carpentry (e.g., plumbing + drywall/painting), or self-perform plumbing directly when holding C-36. Dual B + C-36 licensees (#829071, #1025814, #959843, #1003394, #1115649, #841229) can lawfully execute both the plumbing repair and storage-ceiling drywall restoration under a single contract. VERIFIED CA BPC § 7057 (LegInfo)
16 CCR Div. 8 Art. 3 — CSLB Class C-36
Plumbing Contractor specialty scope		 Covers potable water supply and fluid waste disposal piping, venting, valves, fixtures, and waste-system maintenance/replacement. C-36-only plumbers (#977638, #757796, #1024971) may replace the bathtub waste-and-overflow assembly, but cannot take a standalone drywall restoration contract without subcontracting or pairing with a C-9/B licensee. VERIFIED CSLB Class C-36 Detail
16 CCR Div. 8 Art. 3 — CSLB Class C-9
Drywall Contractor specialty scope		 Covers layout and installation of gypsum wallboard assemblies, nonstructural framing, taping, compounds, and smooth/textured surface finishing. Covers storage-unit ceiling gypsum board replacement, taping, mudding, and texture matching (e.g., Bernal Hill Drywall Inc #995163 holds C-9 + B). VERIFIED CSLB Class C-9 Detail
CA BPC § 7057.5 (SB 1189)
Class B-2 Residential Remodeling Contractor limits		 Requires 3+ unrelated trades in a residential wood-frame structure and prohibits installing, replacing, or substantially altering plumbing systems/mechanisms unless holding C-36 or subcontracting to C-36. A B-2 contractor without C-36 cannot self-perform full concealed waste-and-overflow replacement. VERIFIED CA BPC § 7057.5 (LegInfo)
CSLB Bonding & Down-Payment Rule
Mandatory $25,000 bond, Workers' Comp, and deposit cap		 Active CA contractors must maintain a $25,000 license bond, carry Workers' Compensation if employing workers, and cannot demand a down payment exceeding 10% of contract price or $1,000, whichever is less. Protects property owner in occupied-unit work; verify COI + WC policy if more than the sole owner arrives on site. VERIFIED CSLB — Finding the Right Contractor
