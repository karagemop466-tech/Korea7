Reported Physical Problem (Client Context — Unverified)

Verified Engineering & Licensing Reality (Official Sources)

Official Watco and Oatey bath-waste specifications confirm that a seized brass drop cylinder inside a 17g/20g tubular brass or soldered tee transfers pulling force directly into fragile concealed slip nuts and rear tub gaskets, and post-repair leak testing requires visual inspection behind the tub. Under CA BPC § 7057, a single contractor holding Class B + Class C-36 can execute both the plumbing replacement and ceiling drywall restoration.
