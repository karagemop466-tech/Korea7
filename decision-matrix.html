Neutral official rules

  • SF.gov plumbing permit guidance states that a permit is needed before cutting into or replacing pipes, especially pipes covered by a wall, and that permit fees depend on scope.
  • SF.gov permit-exemption list lists minor repairs to existing interior plaster or wallboard among work that does not need a building permit, subject to exceptions.
  • CSLB B classification and CSLB C-36 classification define the two relevant license scopes. Applicability to the final scope belongs to SF DBI and the selected contractor.

Recommended next steps

  1. Request one contingent written scope from at least three of the five B + C-36 firms.
  2. Require the above-attempt, below-access, ceiling cut/patch/finish, fill-and-inspect test and tenant schedule as separate line items in the same contract.
  3. Verify the actual crew’s workers-compensation and liability coverage on the hire date.
  4. Ask for references on concealed waste-and-overflow work in occupied units; treat company and review statements as unverified until documented.
  5. Re-check the chosen license and complaint disclosure at CSLB Check a License on the day of hiring.
  6. Review all flagged items before signing.
